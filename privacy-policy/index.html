Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. This policy describes exactly what this static version of ClipSetuu does with your information — and, just as importantly, what it does not do.

Official pages — use exactly these links, with one slash:

Home: https://clipsetuu-m4f.vercel.app/
Privacy Policy: https://clipsetuu-m4f.vercel.app/privacy-policy
Terms of Service: https://clipsetuu-m4f.vercel.app/terms

Do not add an extra slash before “privacy-policy”. The older /p/terms address still shows the same Terms page as an alias, but the correct Terms address is /terms.

1. The short version

ClipSetuu in this build is a static web page. There is no server, no database and no account system behind it. Your uploaded videos are never uploaded anywhere — they stay in your own browser. The only things saved are small settings in your browser's local storage on your own device, which you can delete at any time. There is no tracking, no advertising profile, and no sale of personal data.

2. No server upload — your videos stay in your browser

  • When you upload a video file, it is read only by your browser (using a local object URL) so it can be played, framed, captioned and exported on your device.
  • The file is never sent to ClipSetuu, to Vercel, or to any other server by this page. There is no upload endpoint in this build.
  • Export (MP4 / WebM) is also done in your browser. The exported file is created on your device and downloaded by you. Nothing is stored online by ClipSetuu.
  • Closing the tab or refreshing the page ends access to the uploaded file. You will need to upload it again if you want to work on it in a new session.

3. What is stored in your browser only (localStorage)

Only the following are stored, in your browser's local storage on your device, and only if you use the relevant feature:

  • Demo sign-in session: the display name and email address you type into the demo login form. This is a demo only — see section 5. Signing out clears it.
  • My Saved Clips: the title, caption, hashtags, cover hook, timing and cover image for clips you choose to save, plus the name shown on them. Saved clips are kept only in this browser.
  • Custom brand logo: the logo image you upload with “Upload Brand Logo”, so it stays across reloads in this browser.
  • Language choice: the language you pick in the header switcher.

No passwords are stored. The demo email login does not check or store a password anywhere, and nothing you type there is sent to a server. To delete all of the above, use “Delete” on saved clips, “Sign out”, “Reset to default logo”, and clear site data / local storage for this site in your browser settings.

4. No cookies for tracking, no analytics, no ads

This build sets no tracking cookies, runs no analytics, shows no advertisements, and builds no profile about you. Your browser may still store ordinary technical data (such as cached files) as it does for any website, but ClipSetuu does not use it to track you across sites.

5. Demo login — not real Google OAuth

The “Continue with Google” button in this build is a demo sign-in. It does not connect to Google, does not use Google OAuth, does not access your Google account, Gmail, YouTube channel or any other account, and does not receive or store any Google token. The name and email you enter are stored only in your browser, as described above. Real Google sign-in, if it is added later, will be a separate Phase 2 change with its own consent screen, and this policy will be updated before it goes live.

6. Demo publishing — nothing is posted

The “1-Click Upload to YouTube Shorts” and “1-Click Upload to Instagram Reels” buttons open demo preparation modals. They do not post, upload or schedule anything to YouTube or Instagram, do not access any YouTube or Instagram account, and do not send your clip, title, caption, hashtags or cover anywhere. Live posting would need real YouTube / Meta OAuth connections and a backend — that is not in this build.

7. Video links — preview only

Pasting a video link (for example a YouTube link) is used only in your browser to show a title/thumbnail and a timing plan. A link alone cannot be clipped in a browser, and this page does not download the linked video. To cut real footage, upload the video file itself. Your pasted link is not sent to a ClipSetuu server, because there is none.

8. Third-party services your browser may contact

  • Google Fonts: this page loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore contacts Google's font servers when the page loads, and Google may log the usual technical details of that request (such as your IP address) under Google's own privacy policy.
  • YouTube thumbnails: if you paste a YouTube link, your browser may fetch its thumbnail image from YouTube's image server (i.ytimg.com). That request goes directly from your browser to YouTube.

No other third-party service is used by this build to collect your data.

9. Your responsibility for videos you use

Only upload and clip videos you own or have the right to use. You are responsible for respecting copyright, privacy and platform rules for any video, image, music or text you put into a clip, and for anything you later publish from an exported clip. See the Terms of Service.

10. Children

This tool is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because this build has no server, it does not collect personal information from anyone in the first place.

11. Changes to this policy

If this build changes — for example, if real login or real publishing is added — this policy will be updated on this page before that change goes live, with a new “Last updated” date. The version on this page is the version that applies to this deployment.

12. Contact

ClipSetuu in this build has no contact form and collects no messages. If you have a privacy question about a particular deployment, contact the operator of that deployment through the channel where you found the site, and mention the exact site address shown above. To remove your local data right now, clear this site's data in your browser — no request to us is needed, because the data is only on your device.
