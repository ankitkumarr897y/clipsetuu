1. What ClipSetuu is

ClipSetuu is a free, browser-based tool that helps you turn a long video into shorter vertical clips, with animated captions, a cover frame, a virality score, social copy and watermark-free MP4/WebM export. This build is a static page: there is no server, no database and no account backend. Everything — playback, clip timing, captions, thumbnails, copy and export — runs in your browser on your device.

2. Free and unlimited — with honest limits

  • Use of this build is free, with no pricing page, no paywall, no credit counter and no subscription in this version.
  • “Unlimited” means there is no artificial clip counter in this build. It does not mean unlimited server processing, storage or publishing, because this build has no server at all.
  • Very long videos (up to 7 hours are accepted as input) still depend on your browser, device memory and speed. Long exports run in real time in your browser and may be slow or fail on low-memory devices. Export long clips in shorter parts if needed.
  • The service may be unavailable if the hosting deployment is down. No uptime guarantee is given for this free static deployment.

3. Demo features — do not rely on them as live services

  • Demo login: “Continue with Google” and email sign-in are demos. They do not verify your identity, do not connect to Google, and do not prove ownership of any account. Saved clips are stored only in your browser.
  • Demo publishing: the YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels buttons prepare a publish payload in a demo modal. They do not post, upload or schedule anything, and they do not connect to any YouTube or Instagram account. Live posting needs real OAuth and a backend (Phase 2).
  • Demo detection and scoring: moment detection, transcription, captions and virality scores in this build are generated client-side from the title and timings you provide. They are suggestions to help you edit — not real AI analysis of the video's audio or content, and not a promise that a clip will perform well.
